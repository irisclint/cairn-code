# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to causeway to be a good experience for everyone, regardless
of experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most misunderstandings are misunderstandings.
- Give useful feedback. "This will break on Windows because paths use
  backslashes" helps; "this is wrong" does not.
- Accept that maintainers have to say no sometimes, and that a no about scope
  is not a judgement about you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers through a private security advisory on the
repository, or by email to the address in the repository profile. Reports are
handled confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow this code of conduct can be reported the same
way, and the report will be handled by the other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that break
this code. Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent ban.
